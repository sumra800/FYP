## 🚀 How to Use
1. Click "Create Custom Avatar" button on profile page
2. **Select your gender** (Female 👩 or Male 👨) as the first step
3. Use category tabs to navigate different customization options
4. Click on any option to apply it to your avatar
5. Use "🎲 Randomize" button for random combinations (includes gender)
6. Click "Save Avatar" to set as your profile picture
